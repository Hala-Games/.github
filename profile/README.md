## 👋 Who We Are

**HALA GAMES** is a Vietnam-based mobile game studio focused on creating engaging, accessible, and memorable experiences for players around the world.

We develop and publish **casual, puzzle, and hybrid-casual games**, combining creative ideas, thoughtful game design, and continuous iteration to make every play session enjoyable.

We believe great games emerge where **creativity, technology, and player insights** meet.

## 🧩 What We Do

- **Game Development** — Build and refine mobile gameplay experiences, from prototypes to published games.
- **Game Design** — Craft intuitive mechanics, satisfying challenges, and rewarding progression.
- **Data-Informed Iteration** — Learn from player behavior and feedback to improve the experience.
- **Technology & Engineering** — Develop reliable, performant game experiences and the tools that support them.

## 💻 Engineering at HALA GAMES

Our work brings together gameplay engineering, mobile performance, experimentation, and the systems behind game development.

We value:

- **Player-first thinking** — Technology serves the player experience.
- **Practical innovation** — Experiment, measure, learn, and improve.
- **Quality & performance** — Deliver smooth, dependable experiences.
- **Collaboration & ownership** — Share knowledge and take responsibility for what we build.
- **Continuous learning** — Stay curious and keep improving our craft.

Our game development work includes **Unity and C#**. This GitHub organization is a home for the projects, tools, and technical resources we choose to share publicly.
